Modern Villa

A contemporary family residence balancing warm materials, generous daylight and a strong connection to the landscape.

Location
Trivandrum
Scope
Architecture + Interiors
Status
Completed

The brief

The goal was to create a modern family home that feels calm, connected and generous without losing warmth. The planning prioritizes daylight, natural movement and flexible spaces for everyday life.

A restrained material palette lets texture and light create the character. Large openings strengthen the relationship between the interiors and the surrounding landscape.

The result

From the entry sequence to the living spaces and private zones, every part of the home is designed to feel cohesive — contemporary in expression, comfortable in use and personal to the family.
